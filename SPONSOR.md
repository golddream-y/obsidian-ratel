## Why I built this

I live in linked notes in Obsidian. What I wanted was not “another chat box,” but a **graph-native assistant** that can answer from the vault, cite sources, and do multi-step work with controlled edits.

Many tools are either generic chat or push notes to the cloud. I care about:

- **Graph-native** — citations and links that open the real note  
- **Local-first** — local embeddings by default; no telemetry  
- **Your models** — DeepSeek / Claude / Ollama; keys in Obsidian Keychain  

Ratel is that experiment as an open-source Obsidian plugin. Most of the work — coding, store review, edge cases — happens in spare time.
